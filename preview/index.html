2024 / MUSIC2024

Music as a representation

What does a song look like
to a model?

Music became a place to explore representations: fingerprints, embeddings, and the relationships between recordings. Music2vec evolved from Chromaprint2Vec, based on Chromaprint and MusicBrainz, to Harmony2Vec, using CoverHunterMPS and chord progression tools.

The questions became things I could inspect. Map songs into a space. Compare them. Listen to what the similarities reveal—and what they miss.
